## FEW-SHOT EXAMPLES

### Example 1 — Single table aggregation

Task: "Tổng doanh thu theo region trong tháng 3/2024, chỉ tính đơn hoàn thành"

Output:
SELECT
    region,
    ROUND(SUM(amount)) AS total_revenue
FROM orders
WHERE EXTRACT(MONTH FROM order_date) = 3
  AND year = 2024
  AND status IN ('completed', 'processing')
GROUP BY region
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC

### Example 2 — Multi-table JOIN with CTE

Task: "Top 5 sản phẩm bán chạy nhất Q4 2024 theo doanh thu, kèm tên và category"

Output:
WITH q4_sales AS (
    SELECT
        product_id,
        SUM(amount)   AS total_revenue,
        SUM(quantity) AS total_qty
    FROM orders
    WHERE year = 2024
      AND quarter = 4
      AND status IN ('completed', 'processing')
    GROUP BY product_id
)
SELECT
    p.name,
    p.sku,
    p.category,
    q.total_revenue,
    q.total_qty
FROM q4_sales q
JOIN products p ON q.product_id = p.id
ORDER BY q.total_revenue DESC
LIMIT 5
